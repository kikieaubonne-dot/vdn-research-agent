## Étapes reproductibles

Instructions extraites automatiquement : procedure partielle, ordre et fonctionnement non verifies.

1. Im trying to import this bed model into studio from blender and it says triangle count is to high, ive already decimated it and tried importing it in seperate parts but its still in the millions -- [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-reduce-triangle-count/2060610)
2. I recommend making a more low poly version/simplified version of it and then baking the normals for it (use surface appearances to apply it to Roblox), there are great tutorials on Youtube like blender guru which i recommend How to Bake Perfect Normals in Blender - Tutorial - YouTube, How to Bake Normals in 1 minute Blender Tutorial - YouTube -- [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-reduce-triangle-count/2060610)
3. you could decimate a lot of those polygons by hand (selecting the faces and right clicking and then clicking decimate to turn it into 1 face), use the decimate modifier, bake normals on a more low poly version of it -- [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-reduce-triangle-count/2060610)
