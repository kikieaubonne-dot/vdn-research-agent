3. The import process supports both basic textures and realistic PBR textures, as long as they meet Roblox's [texture specifications](/docs/en-us/art/modeling/texture-specifications.md). -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/assign-textures.md)
4. Verify that your object has a Material assigned with the Surface set to `Principled BSDF`. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/assign-textures.md)
5. > **Success:** After replacing your PBR textures, [export the entire model](/docs/en-us/art/modeling/export-requirements.md#blender) and follow instructions to [import into Studio](/docs/en-us/studio/importer.md) -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/assign-textures.md)
