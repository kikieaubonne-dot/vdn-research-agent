## Étapes reproductibles

Instructions extraites automatiquement : procedure partielle, ordre et fonctionnement non verifies.

1. - [Set up Blender](#set-up-blender) to Studio's relative scene units before importing a model to rig. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/rig-a-simple-mesh.md)
2. - [Create and reposition bones](#create-a-bone-structure) within the mesh object. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/rig-a-simple-mesh.md)
3. For this example, import a [robot model](../../assets/modeling/meshes/reference-files/shoebot-base-model.fbx) into Blender as your mesh object. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/rig-a-simple-mesh.md)
4. Now that your model is within Blender, you must add an **armature** and **bones** to your mesh object. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/rig-a-simple-mesh.md)
5. After you add in an armature, you can create and reposition any number of bones inside of your mesh object. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/rig-a-simple-mesh.md)
6. Press `G` and use your mouse to drag the bone onto the **right arm**. -- [Documentation Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/rig-a-simple-mesh.md)
